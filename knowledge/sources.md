# Sources

Compiled for the Fly100 Paraglider Upgrade Readiness app. All summaries elsewhere in `knowledge/` are paraphrased in our own words — this file is the link/attribution index. "Accessed" dates are when this research pass was done (October 2026); "published" dates are given where the source itself states one.

Type key: **official** (certifying body / national association / FAI-CIVL), **manufacturer**, **industry** (magazines, retailers, schools, brand-adjacent blogs), **expert** (named instructor/coach/researcher with a track record), **community** (forums — used only to evidence disagreement, never as a criterion source).

## Official — certification standards

- [BHPA — EN paraglider flight safety characteristics standard (PDF)](https://www.bhpa.co.uk/pdf/En_PG_Classes.pdf) — official, undated PDF, accessed Oct 2026. Defines EN/LTF classes A–D. Note: could not be text-extracted directly by our tooling; content below is reconstructed from indexed/cached text, so treat the exact phrasing as unverified and re-check before quoting anything from it.
- [DHPC — Advice to pilots about choosing wings within the EN classes (PDF)](https://www.dhpc.org.uk/assets/files/Exam%20Revision%20Material/Glider%20ratings%20-%20en926.pdf) — official (devon & cornwall-focused but reproducing the national EN926 standard), undated, accessed Oct 2026. Same extraction caveat as above.
- [DHV — Classification](https://www.dhv.de/en/type-inspection/classification/) — official (German certifying body), accessed Oct 2026. Confirms LTF class definitions map onto EN A–D.
- [Thermal Tracker Paragliding — glider rating systems](https://www.thermaltracker.com/glider-ratings.html) — industry/expert compilation, accessed Oct 2026. Useful cross-reference table of EN vs LTF vs legacy DHV 1/1-2/2/2-3/3 numeric scale.
- [FlyWise — Paraglider EN Rating Guide: A·B·C·D and CCC](https://www.flywise.cloud/en/blog/en-rating-guide) — industry, accessed Oct 2026.
- [Adventuro — Paraglider EN Ratings: A, B, C & CCC Explained](https://adventuro.com/blog/paraglider-wing-en-ratings-and-how-to-stay-safe) — industry, accessed Oct 2026 (referenced indirectly via search synthesis, not deep-fetched).
- [FAI/CIVL — CCC (CIVL Competition Class) requirements PDF](https://www.fai.org/sites/default/files/civl/documents/sporting_code_s7_g_-_ccc_paragliders_requirements_2020_1.0_0.pdf) — official (international), accessed Oct 2026. Technical spec only (aspect ratio ≤7.9, speedbar travel ≤18cm, reduced reaction-time test window), not pilot criteria.
- [FAI — A brief history of serial vs competition class paragliders](https://www.fai.org/news/brief-history-serial-vs-competition-class-paragliders) — official, accessed Oct 2026.

## Explicitly rejected as sources

We found several "paragliding accident statistics" aggregator sites (e.g. wifitalents.com, zipdo.co, gitnux.org) surfaced repeatedly in search results. These look like SEO/content-farm sites repackaging unsourced or AI-generated statistics without clear primary citations. We did not use them anywhere in this knowledge base and recommend the app never cite them either.

[Paraclinic Aotearoa — The Hidden Risk in Paragliding: Ego, Mindset & the Psychology of Safety] was removed after an earlier research pass cited it. The owner (Grant) identified that the article's content is lifted from his own YouTube video transcripts without attribution. It is not an independent source and must not be cited anywhere in this knowledge base or the app — doing so would launder his own stolen material back to him as if it were third-party corroboration. If the underlying ideas (e.g. task- vs ego-oriented motivation shaping upgrade decisions) are wanted in the app, they should come directly from Grant's own material, not this site.
